About Me

I'm a Product Manager for Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Security within Customer Experience Engineering (CxE). With deep roots at Microsoft, I connect engineering teams with the global technical community by turning real customer conversations into actionable insights, technical guidance, and engagement that actually helps people get their work done.

I lead social media and community: we unblock people without a ticket, then take what we hear back into product and support. The rest of the job sits between teams—translating feedback, identifying patterns, and helping shape solutions that scale.

Before Microsoft, I worked my way up from systems administrator in Hawaiʻi through desktop support and service technician roles. That hands-on background gives me a practical perspective on the customer journey—from the person racking the server to the person deploying the endpoint.
